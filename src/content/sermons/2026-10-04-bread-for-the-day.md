In the middle of the Lord's Prayer is a short request about bread. It is a prayer for today, not for the next ten years.

**Three things to take home**

1. Asking for daily bread admits that we depend on God and on each other.
2. It is also a prayer for people who do not have enough. We do not get to pray it for ourselves only.
3. Trusting for today frees us from carrying tomorrow all at once.

**Questions for a small group**

- What is one thing you are carrying that belongs to tomorrow?
- How can our food pantry shelves answer this prayer for someone this week?
