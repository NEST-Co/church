The prayer does not begin with "my." Before we ask for anything, we are placed in a family.

**Three things to take home**

1. Prayer is not a private performance.
2. Calling God "Father" can be tender for some of us and hard for others, and both are welcome here.
3. Praying in the plural pulls us toward each other.

**Questions for a small group**

- What word in the prayer do you find yourself skipping over?
- Who prays with you regularly?
