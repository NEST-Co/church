The writer of Hebrews tells us not to neglect hospitality, because some have welcomed angels without knowing it.

**Three things to take home**

1. Hospitality starts small: a chair pulled out, a name remembered.
2. Welcome costs something. It takes time and a willingness to be interrupted.
3. We do not have to be experts. We only have to show up and say hello.

**Questions for a small group**

- Who welcomed you the first time you walked into a new place?
- What is one small thing we could change on Sunday mornings?
