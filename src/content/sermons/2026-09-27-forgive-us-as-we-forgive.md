We ask to be forgiven in the same sentence where we promise to forgive. That is a hard line, and it deserves a slow reading.

**Three things to take home**

1. Forgiveness is not pretending nothing happened.
2. It is a decision that can take many tries.
3. Being forgiven is what makes forgiving others possible.

**Questions for a small group**

- Is there someone you have been meaning to forgive?
- What helps you keep going when forgiving feels slow?
