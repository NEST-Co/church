The church library lives in the hallway by the office. Here are five kinds of book worth a look this season.

1. A short, readable introduction to a book of the Bible.
2. A memoir from someone who changed their mind about something big.
3. A children's picture book that adults enjoy too.
4. A book of poems, for reading one at a time.
5. A practical book about prayer from a tradition you do not know well.

Ask at the office if you want help finding one. Borrowing is on the honor system.
