The youth fall retreat is November 13 to 15 at a camp about an hour away. It is open to grades 7 to 12, and friends are welcome.

**What to bring:** a sleeping bag, warm clothes, a water bottle and a flashlight.

**Cost:** the retreat is free to families. Nobody is turned away.

**Sign-up:** return the paper form to the youth team, or ask at the office. Forms are due November 6.

Adult volunteers are needed. Background checks are required and we will walk you through them.
