Park in the lot on Elm Street. A greeter at the front door will point you toward the sanctuary, the children's rooms and the restrooms.

The service runs about an hour. We sing a few songs, hear a reading and a talk, and pray together. You do not have to stand, sing or say anything you do not want to.

Children are welcome in the service. There is also a nursery and a Sunday school during the talk.

Afterward there is coffee in the hall. Stay as long as you like.
