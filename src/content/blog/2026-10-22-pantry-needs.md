Our pantry serves about forty households a week. These items run out first: peanut butter, canned fruit, cereal and diapers in sizes 4 to 6.

**Ways to help**

- Drop items in the bins by the front doors any day the office is open.
- Give an hour on Thursday afternoons. We always need people to restock shelves.
- Tell a neighbor. People do not always know the pantry is open to everyone.

No sign-up is needed to receive food. Come on Thursday between 4 and 6 p.m.
