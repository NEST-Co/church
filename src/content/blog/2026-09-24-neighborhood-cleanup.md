Thanks to everyone who came out on Saturday. Thirty-one people from the church and the neighborhood picked up litter along Elm Street and the creek path.

We filled eleven bags of trash and four of recycling. The best find was a working garden hose.

We are planning to do this again in the spring. If you have a section of street you would like us to cover, tell us.
